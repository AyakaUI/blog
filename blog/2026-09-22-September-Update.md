# September 2026 Update

<Banner src="https://github.com/AyakaUI-CI/blog_assets/raw/refs/heads/main/2026-09-22/banner.webp" />

AyakaUI Seventeen continues to move forward. With the initial Android 17 release behind us, the September update is where things start settling into place, with further polish, fixes, and improvements across the system.

This release should also mark the start of a broader Android 17 rollout across our device roster. More supported devices are expected to receive their first PixelOS Seventeen builds over the coming weeks, as maintainers complete their Android 17 bring-up.

As always, detailed changelogs can be found on our [changelogs page](https://blog-ayaka.vercel.app/changelogs/).

## Other changes
SystemUI: force black shade for black theme
